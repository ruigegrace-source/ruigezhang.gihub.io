Truss Bridge Load Optimization

Course: Intro to Engineering Design | Team Size: 4

The Challenge / Situation

Design a lightweight truss structure constrained by a 400g maximum weight limit capable of supporting a 15kg dynamic load center.

Action & Technical Approach

I calculated theoretical member forces using joint resolution method in MATLAB. Iterated through 3 distinct member configurations to optimize member tension/compression ratios before physical fabrication.

Result & Lessons Learned

The bridge supported 17.5kg before failure (exceeding project target by 16%). Learned key insights regarding joint stress concentrations and physical manufacturing vs. ideal theoretical models.
